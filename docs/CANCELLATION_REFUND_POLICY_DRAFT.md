## Cancellation

Only the workspace owner can cancel a workspace subscription. A cancellation request stops renewal at the end of the current paid period. Paid benefits remain available until that effective date, which the billing interface displays. Repeating a cancellation request is safe and does not move the effective date.

Cancellation does not delete the workspace, members, projects, files, or billing history. Cancellation and refund requests are separate. No early-termination fee or special notice period is introduced by this draft.

## Refunds

Nexus does not issue automatic refunds when a subscription is cancelled, downgraded, or when seats are reduced. Refund requests are reviewed and handled manually under the approved business policy and applicable law. No refund outcome, processing time, or eligibility rule is promised until reviewed and approved.

## Data retention and account deletion

Cancelling a subscription does not delete customer data. Account deletion is a separate authenticated request. The provisional product policy now proposes immediate access revocation without a recovery period, but the current backend still schedules deletion after 30 days. This mismatch must be resolved before customer-facing deletion terms or a deletion interface are published. The proposal preserves eligible shared organization content and requires ownership transfer or workspace restriction when no eligible successor exists. Organization deletion is separate and authorized. These are provisional product rules, not legal approval.
